A little practice, every day.

IncludeMe is a micro-learning app that builds inclusive habits through short, daily exercises. The two tracks let everyone take part in their own way.

  • ScenariosExplore a situation from another perspective.
  • ReflectionsPause and think about what you have learned.
  • Small challengesPractise inclusive behaviour in everyday life.

Track A · For allies

In Their Shoes

Understand what it can feel like to be different.

Through scenarios, reflections, and small challenges, you can practise perspective-taking and inclusive behaviour at your own pace.

Track B · For your perspective

My Voice

Your experience is worth putting into words.

For people with disabilities or personal circumstances, this track helps you articulate your experiences, set boundaries, and share your perspective.
